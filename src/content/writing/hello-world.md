I've finally moved my writing somewhere I control. This is the first post.

## Why a new site

The old `stefanahman.github.io` was a single page with my name and a link to my CV. Good enough for a while — but I want a place to write, and that needs more than one HTML file.

The plan is simple: write things here when I have something worth saying, and keep the rest of the site (work history, projects, eventually photos) one or two clicks away.
