title: Thunder and lightning
description: A love letter on polarity, charge, and the storm that builds in the gap between us.
pubDate: 2026-06-02

---

In the cloud, the charges separate. Negative sinks low and heavy. Positive rises and reaches. The storm is born in the distance between us, in the pull of opposites held apart, longing across the gap.

You are the positive charge, reaching upward, gathering, building potential with every moment before you are met. I am the negative pole, deep and grounded, the dark base of the cloud. I hold, I wait, I gather weight. Alone, we are charge with nowhere to go.

The gap between us is not empty. It pulls at us both. It hums. It holds tension, voltage rising the longer we stay true to our poles. You will not become me. I will not become you. That refusal to collapse into sameness is what builds the charge. The more fully you are positive and I am negative, the more the air between us tightens, toward the moment we can no longer stay apart.

Then the threshold breaks. The longing grew too great to hold. The lightning is the meeting, positive and negative crossing the void in a single white seam, the release of everything held back in the waiting. You are the flash, the visible joy of it. I am the thunder, the deep voice that answers, the whole sky resounding because we met.

The meeting doesn't end us. It empties us, only so we can fill again. Negative sinks, positive rises, the distance opens. The longing rebuilds. The storm is not one strike. It is the endless return of poles parting so we can meet, meeting so we must part.

Apart, we are pure potential. Two charges turning toward each other, drawn tighter the longer we wait. Every time we meet, the sky breaks open with sound and light.
